How to choose the right social media marketer

  1. Ask for real examples. Request live pages, reels and ad results, not only slide decks.
  2. Know who does the work. A specialist you can speak to beats a rotating team.
  3. Demand clear reporting. Reach, leads and cost per lead, shared monthly.
  4. Check organic and paid together. Great content with weak ads, or the reverse, wastes budget.
  5. Start with a defined scope. A written plan and a short trial period protect you.
